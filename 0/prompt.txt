Now i'd like us to consider one final improvement to our Jenkins CI system: sometimes it happens that the baseline doesn't change very much (because we don't merge things into main too frequently), but we are making final changes to the candidate and making multiple commits. Since CI currently takes 2-3 hours, it is a share to have to re-generate baseline results every time, even though baseline hasn't changed. What if we didn't delete the baseline results, and when CI runs it checkes whether baseline results already exist and whether they are from the same baseline ref that we currently intend to use? if they are, don't generate baseline results again (this should speed up some CI runs by roughly 2X). Of course, if baseline results are not available or if baseline has changed, then do generate baseline results.
